# Requirements

Source: interview with the owner (contractor), Malang. Answers below are the owner's; anything not yet answered is listed under _Open questions_.

## Inventory

| Topic             | Answer                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Most common items | Andang besi (steel scaffolding), mesin molen (concrete mixer), engsel (hinges) and other hardware, pipa PVC, other building materials |
| Units used        | buah, m², meter, lonjor                                                                                                               |
| Partial purchase  | **Allowed** — buyers may take part of a stock (e.g. 10 of 50 lonjor)                                                                  |

## Pricing & negotiation

- Prices are **negotiable**. Negotiation happens in WhatsApp chat with the owner, never on the website.
- The website may show a reference price per unit, or "Nego" when no price is set.

## Payment & pickup

- Payment: **bank transfer** or **cash at pickup** (paid right before the goods are taken).
- The website does not process payments in v1; payment info is shown as static text.

## Contact channel

- **Primary WhatsApp:** the owner (Papa) — all chats should go here by default.
- **Backup WhatsApp:** older sibling (Kakak) — used only when the primary is unreachable.

## Buyers

- Mostly **other contractors (pemborong) and construction workers (tukang)**.
- Implications:
  - They know the materials — show specs, quantity, unit, and condition clearly; skip marketing language.
  - They browse on phones, often on site — mobile-first, fast-loading, large tap targets.
  - They may buy in bulk — quantity must be prominent on cards and detail pages.
